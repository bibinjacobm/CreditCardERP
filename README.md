# Personal Credit Card ERP

**Manage. Monitor. Optimize.**

A professional, fully offline Windows application for managing all your credit cards in one place — bills, payments, due dates, rewards, lounge and other benefits, annual fees, EMIs, statements and reports. No cloud, no subscription: your data never leaves your computer.

## Download

👉 Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `CreditCardERP-<version>-Setup.exe`.

Run it — no administrator rights needed. If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## What it does

**Cards, bills and payments**
- All your cards across banks — including cards that **share one credit limit** and **dual cards** (e.g. Visa + RuPay with one bill).
- Statements and payments with automatic status: Pending, Minimum Paid, Partially Paid, Paid in Full, Overpaid, Overdue.
- Split payments, and **Pay Together** — one payment that settled several cards at once.
- **Due-date alerts**, a billing-cycle planner and credit-limit history (including future-dated limit changes).

**Smart statement import**
- Import **statement PDFs** (password-protected too), **scans and photos** (built-in OCR), or **Excel / CSV** files.
- Reads the statement date, due date, total and minimum due and every transaction; **duplicates are detected** for you.
- **Auto Bills:** select many statements at once and the bills are created for you. Every import can be undone.
- After an import, one screen helps you file the PDF, record payments, log rewards and check EMIs.

**Rewards, benefits and fees**
- Reward points and cashback with **expiry alerts**, redemptions and reconciliation with your statement.
- **Lounge, golf, insurance** and other benefits — free uses left, spend conditions to unlock them, and a benefits calendar.
- **Annual fees and waivers** — how much more to spend for a waiver, renewal reminders and retention offers.
- **Card Library** of 73 popular Indian credit cards (ICICI, HDFC, SBI, Axis, Kotak, AU, HSBC, Amex, IDFC FIRST, Federal, YES, IndusInd, RBL and more) — sets up rewards, fees and benefits for your card in one click, with tips on how to use it.

**Planning and insight**
- **EMI Manager:** full EMI schedules matched against your statements, and a simulator that compares tenures and no-cost EMIs before you buy — showing the real cost, including GST on interest.
- **Smart Picks:** which card to use for a purchase, a payment plan for the coming weeks and a financial-year spend forecast.
- **Analytics** and a **Credit Health** score with warnings, suggestions and limit-increase readiness.
- A dashboard you can customise.

**Documents, reports and safety**
- **Statement repository:** your statement PDFs filed automatically by bank, card and financial year — and searchable.
- **20+ reports** with PDF, Excel and CSV export and printing.
- **Automatic, verified backups** — optionally **encrypted** and copied to a second folder (USB drive or cloud folder) — and a restore wizard for moving to a new PC.
- Archive old years or closed cards to keep the app fast. Optional **PIN** at start-up. Light and dark themes, adjustable text size.

## Updates

The app checks for new versions automatically and installs them in a minute. Your data is backed up first and is always kept. You can see what changed in each version, skip a version, or go back to an earlier one from **Settings → Updates**.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Internet is needed only to check for updates (this can be switched off).
